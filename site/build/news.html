JACS

June 18, 2026

Visualizing Millisecond Atomic Dynamics of Nanocrystals in Liquid

Our new Journal of the American Chemical Society paper uses millisecond-speed liquid-cell electron microscopy and deep-learning denoising to observe atomic-scale dynamics in gold nanocrystals in reactive liquids. The study reveals reversible changes in local crystallinity and how nanocrystal–liquid interactions affect dissolution and grain-boundary relaxation.

Read the paper

Published March 11, 2026; featured on the cover of the May 2026 issue

Article featured on the cover of Nature Synthesis

Our article, “Kinetic-controlled transformations of group-III arsenide nanocubes,” reports the cation-exchange transformation of Cu3As nanocubes into InAs and GaAs. The work shows how reaction kinetics guide nucleation, phase propagation, symmetry changes, and the formation of crystalline, multidomain, amorphous, or hollow structures.

June 27, 2025

Recreating George Washington Carver’s blue pigment

In collaboration with artist Amanda Williams, our team adapted a century-old pigment recipe patented by George Washington Carver. Safer acids and modern equipment helped make the Prussian blue pigment reproducibly in hours.
